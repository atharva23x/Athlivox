What is a sponsor partner?

A sponsor partner is an organisation that funds athletes directly - travel, coaching, equipment or tournament fees. Athlivox verifies every partner before listing it. Players can send one request per partner; the Athlivox admin team reviews it and shares approved requests with the sponsor.

Terms & Conditions

You must agree before entering an event.

1. Accurate athlete details. You confirm that the name, email, college, sport, and phone number you submit are true and belong to you.

2. Event participation. Registering reserves a spot for the selected event. You agree to follow the organizer's rules, check-in times, and venue safety requirements.

3. Cancellations. If you cannot attend, cancel from the Events page so the spot is released to another athlete.

4. Data and privacy. Your account details, registrations, and uploaded reels are stored to operate your Athlivox experience and are not shared outside the platform.

5. Content rules. Reels must be your own sports highlights and must not contain harmful or misleading content.
